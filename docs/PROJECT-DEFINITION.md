# Pepthea — project definition

## Product intent

Pepthea is a DTC brand for **cosmetic (topical) peptide skincare and
dietary (ingestible) collagen peptides**. Its single differentiating
promise: *radical transparency* — the active's concentration on the
front of the label, the dose taken from the published studies, and a
third-party certificate of analysis published for every production
lot, findable by the lot number printed on the unit.

Deliberately **not** in scope, ever: injectable, compounded, or
"research use only" peptides. That category is unapproved-drug
territory; processors and ad platforms ban it. The brand's moat is
being the trustworthy option in a category famous for gray-market
sketchiness — `/science` says this out loud.

## Audience

1. **Skincare-literate buyers (25–45)** who read INCI lists, know
   what Matrixyl is, and are frustrated that brands hide doses.
2. **Wellness pragmatists** buying collagen who want heavy-metal
   testing they can actually see.
3. **First-time peptide users** arriving from social content; served
   by the Routine Builder and the "your first peptide" product.

## Positioning

"Peptides that say exactly what they are." Premium-honest, not
premium-luxury: mid-tier pricing ($24–$58 single products), evidence
graded candidly (including where it's thin), no miracle language.
The honesty *is* the brand voice — see the no-fake-reviews section
on the home page.

## Business model

- One-time purchases + **Subscribe & Save 15%** (60-day cycle) —
  consumable products make subscription the core LTV engine.
- Bundle (The Complete Routine) as AOV driver.
- Wholesale channel later (`wholesale@` inbox exists).

## Success metrics (Phase 1)

- Checkout conversion from PDP ≥ 3%
- Subscription share of orders ≥ 25%
- Routine Builder completion ≥ 60% of starts
- Zero compliance incidents (claims, disclaimers, processor terms)

## Constraints

- Copy claims must survive FTC/FDA scrutiny (see CLAUDE.md rule 1).
- Everything must run with zero configuration for preview.
- Payment category requires processor pre-approval care — see
  `docs/PAYMENTS.md`.
